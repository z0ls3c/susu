# Susu Notes

## What is a susu?

- Rotating savings club
- Members contribute an equal amount periodically (weekly or monthly) to a lumpsum
- Lumpsum is distributed to a single member
- Each member will get a turn in getting lumpsum
- Members don't make a profit
- Member who receives an early distribution is effectively getting a loan, but repays as that member still contributes
- Member who receives a late distribution is effectively saving their contributions
- The organizer may be compensated
